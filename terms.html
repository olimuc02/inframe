2. Biometric Data Policy (Face Matching)

In short: we turn your selfie into a face template only so we can find you in your friends' photos. We never sell it, never use it for anything else, and delete it when you ask or leave.

This policy is written to meet biometric privacy laws, including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, Washington's biometric identifier law and similar state laws.

2.1 What We Collect

When you sign up or update your Face Match Selfie, InFrame scans your selfie and creates a face template, a list of numbers that describes your face's geometry. We also keep a small cropped copy of that selfie, which only you can see in your settings. Together these are your "biometric data."

2.2 Why We Collect It

We use your face template for one purpose only: to recognize you in photos uploaded to groups and events you belong to, so those photos can be offered to you. We do not use it for advertising, for identifying you to strangers, for tracking you outside InFrame, or to train any AI model.

2.3 People in Photos Who Aren't Matched

Photos are scanned on the uploading member's own device. Faces in a photo are compared only with the face templates of members of that group or event. InFrame does not save face templates of people in photos. It saves only the location of each face in the photo and which member, if any, it matched. People who aren't InFrame members are never identified.

2.4 Your Consent

We collect your biometric data only after you check the face-matching consent box. Checking that box is your written release under these laws. Face matching is optional after sign-up: you can remove your face data at any time from Profile → Face Match Selfie → Remove My Face Data and still claim photos manually from Uncategorized.

2.5 No Selling or Profiting

We will never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from your biometric data.

2.6 Who Can Access It

Your face template is used by the InFrame app on the devices of members of your groups and events, only to run the matching described above. We store it with our cloud database provider, Google Firebase, which handles it only on our behalf. We won't disclose it to anyone else unless you consent, it's needed to complete a transaction you asked for, or we're required to by law, a court order or a subpoena.

2.7 How Long We Keep It

We permanently destroy your biometric data at the earliest of:

  • when you remove your face data or delete your account (we delete it right away)
  • when the purpose of collecting it has been satisfied
  • 3 years after your last use of InFrame

Backup copies held by our providers, if any, are overwritten on their normal schedule, within 30 days.

2.8 How We Protect It

We store and transmit biometric data using a reasonable standard of care that is at least as protective as how we handle other sensitive personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls.
